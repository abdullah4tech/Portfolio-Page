The major AI language models speak English, Mandarin, Spanish. They barely speak Yoruba, Twi, Krio, or the hundreds of other languages spoken across Africa — because the training data doesn't exist at scale, and what does exist hasn't been rigorously evaluated.

Open MOS is a platform that tackles both sides of that problem: it gathers low-resource African language data from contributors across the continent, then evaluates the quality of that data using Mean Opinion Score (MOS) — the same standard used to measure speech and language quality in research.

## What it solves

- African languages are underrepresented in AI because there's no organized, quality-evaluated dataset
- Existing evaluation methods aren't accessible to researchers without major infrastructure
- No centralized place for contributors to submit and for researchers to access rated African language data

## How it works

- Contributors submit audio samples, text, or transcriptions in their native language
- Evaluators rate submissions using a structured MOS framework
- Aggregated scores give researchers a reliable quality signal for each language sample
- Data is openly available for use in model training and linguistic research

## Highlights

- Supports multiple African languages with more added as contributors join
- MOS evaluation pipeline built for non-expert evaluators
- Open dataset — free for academic and research use
- Built with African NLP researchers and contributors in mind
