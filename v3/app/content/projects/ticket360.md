Most small support teams are still managing customer complaints through phone calls, WhatsApp messages, and handwritten notes. Issues get lost, nothing is tracked, and there's no visibility across departments. Ticket360 fixes that.

It gives organizations a central place where every customer complaint becomes a tracked ticket — logged, assigned, and resolved. Support agents can escalate issues to other departments with a single click, no phone tag, no walking across the office. Everyone sees the same state.

## What it solves

- Customers submit issues through the platform instead of calling — every complaint is on record
- Support teams get a clear queue of open tickets with priority and status
- Issues route to the right department instantly — engineering, billing, logistics, whoever needs it
- Management gets visibility into resolution times and recurring problems

## Highlights

- Multi-tenant workspaces — each organization runs in isolation
- Real-time ticket updates via WebSocket
- Department-based routing and escalation in one click
- Role-based access: agents, supervisors, and admins see what they need
- SLA tracking and resolution metrics dashboard
