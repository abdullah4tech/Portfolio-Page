Most period tracking apps are glorified calendars. They log dates and predict cycles but have no idea what's actually going on with you. Gadnet is built differently — it uses an AI agent that learns from your personal cycle history and turns that data into real, contextual insight.

If you're feeling pain on day 14, Gadnet doesn't give you a generic article. It looks at your logged history, recognizes the pattern, tells you what's likely happening, and — when something doesn't add up — tells you it's time to see a doctor.

## What it solves

- Women struggle to connect their daily symptoms to their cycle phase
- Generic apps can't answer personal health questions with personal data
- There's no lightweight way to get informed guidance without a doctor visit for every question

## How it works

- An AI agent is trained on the user's logged cycle data over time
- Ask it anything — "why am I feeling this way?" or "is this normal for day 3?" — it answers from your data
- Symptom logging feeds the agent so answers get more accurate over time
- Clear escalation signal: when a pattern suggests a medical issue, it says so

## Highlights

- AI agent with full cycle context — not a general-purpose health chatbot
- Symptom-to-phase correlation across logged history
- Smart doctor referral prompts when patterns are abnormal
- Clean, private — data stays on-device and in your account
